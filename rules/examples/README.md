# Examples (real posts from the dashboard)

Transcribed from screenshots of real posts. Only these examples are used for now.

## How to use them
- `style-guide.md` DECIDES the structure and length (3 to 4 paragraphs for news). The examples are NOT the length model: real posts are often shorter.
- Use the examples for: real wording of official names, titles and academic titles, how each kind of event is described, and which Structure and Category Dep values exist.
- Each file has a Quality line. Copy the shape of GOOD MODEL files. Take only facts, names or categories from the others.

## Never copy from any example
- The title repeated as the first line of the body.
- A "we" voice, invitations or appeals to the public.
- Stray `**` marks, sentences run together, a space after the letter و, or a missing full stop at the end of a paragraph.
- Names, numbers and details of the people in the examples. They belong to those posts only.

## SDG paragraph examples
- Examples 11 and 12 show how a real post links its activity to sustainability goals in the closing paragraph. Copy the shape and the level of generality of that paragraph. See "Sustainability goals" in GEMINI.md.
